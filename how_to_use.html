How to play

Four steps.
One commitment.

Every case scenario runs the same loop. Read this once before your first challenge — the rules on locking and scoring are what make it worth playing.

STEP 01

The scenario

Read the patient's history, presentation and referral note. This is everything you get for free.

STEP 02

Investigation

Select physical examinations and diagnostic tests. You may pick several — but each choice is final.

STEP 03

Management

Synthesise your findings and commit to the single most indicated management strategy from four options.

STEP 04

Result

Compare every selection against the gold standard, with a written rationale for each option and an overall accuracy score.

Step 02 in detail

Investigative logic

  • Choose physical examinations or diagnostic tests. You can pick multiple options.

  • One-way choice. Once you click an option it is confirmed, and the findings are revealed immediately. There is no undo.

  • Scoring. Marks are deducted for over-investigating with unnecessary tests, and for under-investigating when you miss vital information.
