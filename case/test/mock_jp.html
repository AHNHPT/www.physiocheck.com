Physiotherapy Clinical Competency Check

A 30-item written assessment adapted in format from a national physiotherapist registration examination, covering orthopaedics, neurology, paediatrics, cardiopulmonary, geriatrics, anatomy/physiology, professional conduct and psychiatry topics relevant to day-to-day clinical practice.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (1–5). Most questions require you to select one answer; questions marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item will be marked as incorrect for that item — the same rule used in the source examination.
  3. You may move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous/Next buttons before submitting.
  4. Once you submit, you'll see your score, a marked answer sheet, and a full explanation for every question.
