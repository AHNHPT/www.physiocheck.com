🧪 Key Points

  • Lithium is a key mood stabilizer with a narrow therapeutic index, meaning the safe dose is very close to a toxic dose. Dehydration or electrolyte imbalances can quickly trigger lithium toxicity because the body holds onto lithium when salt or water levels drop.
