PneumoSim 01 Live

Airway clearance in severe pneumonia

Suction and airway clearance on a deteriorating ICU patient. Read the vitals, act, and watch the physiology answer back.
PneumoSim 02 Live

Early mobilisation of the ventilated patient

Mobilising a ventilated patient with an isolated inhalation burn — line and circuit management under real-time deterioration.
FallSim 01 Live

Witnessed patient fall in OPD

A fall with head strike during gait re-training. DRABC, vitals and TOTAPS, escalation, SBAR and incident documentation.
PaedSim 01 Live

Nasopharyngeal suction in a child with hMPV

Catheter depth, calibre and vacuum time in a 4-year-old with BPD and cerebral palsy. Every technique error has a consequence.

Simulators run in real time and are best on a laptop or tablet with sound on. Allow 20–30 minutes each.
