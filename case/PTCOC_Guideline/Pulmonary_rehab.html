Inclusion Criteria

Priority Order (Central Committee 2024)

  • 1 COPD patients with 1 hospitalization due to AECOPD within the past year.
  • 2 COPD patients with ≥ 2 hospitalizations due to AECOPD within the past year.
  • 3 Symptomatic chronic chest patients with mMRC dyspnoea score > 2.
Milder Disease Protocol (mMRC ≤ 2): Patients refusing comprehensive PRP with mMRC ≤ 2 (and not on LTOT) should be referred to HA community support at primary care level.

Exclusion Criteria

Strict Contraindications

  • cardiac or non-respiratory disease (e.g. dementia, severe arthritis, severe back pain) that prevents exercise or attendance.
  • End-stage respiratory disease with extremely poor rehabilitative potential (e.g., bedbound).
  • Poor motivation or patient non-compliance.
  • Completed comprehensive PRP within the previous one year.

Timing & Modality Settings

Hospital Discharge Timing: Within 4 weeks (GOLD 2025/BTS 2023)
Clinically Stable Referrals: Initiate within 3 months of receiving referral.
Standard Program Duration: At least 6 weeks, including a minimum of twice weekly supervised sessions.
Alternative Options: Tele-rehabilitation is a valid alternative for patients declining supervised centre-based programs, provided visual/fall risk is low.

Dyspnoea Control Strategies

Pursed-Lip Breathing (PLB): Reduces intrinsic PEEP and dynamic hyperinflation, alters muscle recruitment pattern, and decreases respiratory rate (Breslin, 1992).
Diaphragmatic Breathing (DB): Reduces sympathetic activity (Zare et al., 2025). *Precaution:* Contraindicated in patients with severe COPD and marked hyperinflation without adequate diaphragmatic excursion.
Rollator Fixation: Fixing arms on a rollator in a forward lean position stabilizes the upper limb and shoulder girdle, optimizing the accessory muscles' pressure-generating capacity.
Acupressure & Acu-TENS: Key targets for breathlessness: EX-B1 (Ding Chuan), BL-13, BL-23, ST-36, LU-1, LU-7 (Wei et al., 2022).

Airway Clearance / Bronchial Hygiene

Active vs. Passive Techniques:

  • Teach Active Cycle of Breathing (ACBT), autogenic drainage, and selectively direct huffing. Active techniques are highly preferred over passive.
  • Crucial Hazard Warning: Manual chest and abdominal compression increases peak cough flow in muscular weakness, but decreases peak cough flow in COPD patients due to airway collapse! (Sivasothy et al, 2001).
  • Physical Exercise Effect: Resistance training (weights/elastic tubing) significantly improves mucociliary clearance (Silva et al., 2019). Aerobic training alone has no significant effect on clearance.

Aerobic Training

  • Frequency: 3-5 days per week (minimum 2 supervised sessions).
  • Duration: 20-60 minutes per session.
  • Intensity:
    • 60-80% of peak work rate on cycle stress test.
    • For walking, use 60-80% speed calculated from 6MWT.
    • Symptom-limited: Target Borg RPD of 4 to 6 ("somewhat severe" to "severe").

Resistance Training

  • Frequency: 2-3 days per week.
  • Intensity: 50-85% of 1 Repetition Maximum (1RM).
  • Sets & Reps: 2-4 sets of 8-12 repetitions per muscle group.
  • Execution Speed: Moderate control: 1-2s concentric, 1-2s eccentric phases.
  • Muscle Groups: Major upper & lower limbs (quadriceps, biceps, pectoralis, traps).

Inspiratory Muscle (IMT)

  • Role: Adjunct intervention only. Does not replace routine whole-body PRP exercises.
  • Frequency: 5-7 days per week.
  • Intensity: Start at > 30% of MIP; progress incrementally (5-10% weekly) to 45-60% of MIP.
  • Pacing: ~8 breaths/min (Inspiration: ~2s; Expiration: ~6s). Borg rating of 4-6.

Advanced Rehab Adjuncts

Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES): Excellent for frail/severely dyspnoeic patients in acute phases. Bypasses breathlessness and central cardiac loads. Frequency: 50-120Hz; Duty cycle: 2-10s on, 4-50s off; 20-60 mins/day.
Whole Body Vibration (WBV): Squats with WBV (Side-alternating: >20Hz; Vertical: <35Hz; displacement >4mm) improve 6MWD & stand speed while minimizing systemic fatigue.
Eccentric Training & Downhill Walking: Generates up to 4x higher muscle force with lower cardiopulmonary / ventilatory demands compared to standard cycle/treadmill protocols.

Special Non-COPD Conditions

Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD): Focus on pacing/energy conservation. High-flow oxygen is common. Monitor gas exchange closely.
Pulmonary Hypertension (PH): Keep SpO2 > 88%. Strictly avoid Valsalva-like maneuvers. Remotely supervised exercise is NOT recommended due to syncope/arrhythmia risks.
Cystic Fibrosis (CF): Separate patients based on respiratory pathogens in gym sessions. Monitor blood sugar (CF-related diabetes) and fluids.
Lung Transplantation: Monitor Sat carefully. SAT should be >90% during exercise; if SpO2 drops below 88%, rule out acute rejection/allograft dysfunction immediately.
