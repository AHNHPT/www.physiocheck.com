Section 1

Introduction, Classification & Epidemiology

TOP

📋 Classification of DM

Type 2 DM (T2DM)
90–95% of cases • Insulin resistance + secretory defect
Type 1 DM (T1DM)
5–10% of cases • Autoimmune pancreatic β-cell destruction
Gestational DM
Screened at 24–28 weeks in pregnancy

📍 Hong Kong Situation (2023)

  • • Prevalence: ~8.5% among adults aged 15-48
  • • 54.1% undiagnosed
  • • 490,000+ T2DM patients under HA care
  • • 11th leading cause of death

Section 2

Benefits of Exercise

Aerobic Exercise

  • ↓ HbA1c 0.14% for every 100 min/week physical activity.
  • ↑ Insulin sensitivity
  • Improved VO₂ max
  • Cardiovascular protection

Resistance Training

  • ↑ Muscle mass & strength
  • Enhanced glucose uptake
  • Better BP & lipid profile
  • Counter sarcopenia

Additional Benefits

  • HIIT: Greater cardiometabolic gains
  • Balance: ↓ Fall risk (DM patient is 2.3x more likely to fall)
  • Overall: ↓ Morbidity & mortality
Section 3

Exercise Prescription

150 min moderate / week

Aerobic Recommendations

Type
Consider personal interest (eg.Walking, cycling, swimming, aerobic dance)
Intensity
Moderate (40-60% HRR) to Vigorous (60-90% HRR)
Duration / Frequency
150 min/week moderate • 75 min/week vigorous • ≥5 days/week moderate
Key Tip: No more than 2 consecutive days without aerobic exercise to maintain insulin sensitivity.

Resistance Training

🏋️
2–3 non-consecutive days/week
🔁
8–10 exercises • 1–3 sets of 10–15 reps
📈
Progress from moderate to vigorous

Flexibility & Balance

  • Static/dynamic stretches 2–3 days/week
  • Hold 10–30 seconds, 2–4 reps
  • Tai Chi or single-leg stands for older adults
  • Reduce fall risk
⚡

High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

Promising alternative for cardiometabolic benefits

Key Evidence
  • • Effective for improving HbA1c, peak VO₂ and cardiometabolic risk factors
  • • Greater benefits than moderate continuous training in some studies
  • • Improves peripheral artery stiffness and carotid distensibility
Practical Considerations
Protocol
Alternate short bursts of high intensity with recovery periods. Example: 4x4 min intervals
Suitability
For stable patients. Start with supervision. Not for high-risk or deconditioned individuals initially.
Optimum protocol still evolving. Equivalent benefit to moderate training in many patients.

Special Considerations & Precautions

Glycemic Control
  • <3.9 mmol/L: Relative contraindication
  • <5.6 mmol/L: Consider carb intake
  • >16.7 mmol/L: Perform Urine Ketone + Ask Symptoms + Hydration
  • >19.4 mmol/L: Conservative insulin if needed
Neuropathy & Retinopathy
  • Avoid rapid postural changes
  • No high BP elevating activities if retinopathy
  • Foot care & proper footwear
Screening & Progression
  • Use ACSM pre-participation screening
  • Gradual progression from light-moderate
  • Monitor RPE & Talk Test
