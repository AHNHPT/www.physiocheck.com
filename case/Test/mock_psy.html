Psychiatric Physiotherapy Competency Check

A 30-item self-assessment for junior physiotherapy staff, covering core psychiatric knowledge and case-based clinical reasoning relevant to day-to-day practice on psychiatric wards — medication side effects, risk recognition, safe handling, professional boundaries, and person-centred care.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (1–5). Most questions require you to select one answer; questions marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item will be marked as incorrect for that item.
  3. Questions alternate between knowledge items (definitions, facts, principles) and case-based items (a short clinical vignette followed by a question).
  4. You may move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous/Next buttons before submitting.
  5. Once you submit, you'll see your score, a marked answer sheet, and a full explanation for every question.
