Psychiatric Physiotherapy Competency Check

A 30-item assessment for junior physiotherapy staff working with psychiatric in-patients and out-patients: symptom recognition, medication effects that change how we treat, ward safety, communication, boundaries, and management of specific presentations. Half the items test knowledge, half are clinical vignettes. This is a hard paper by design — on most items more than one option is defensible, and you are being asked for the strongest one.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (1–5). Most require one answer; items marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item is marked as incorrect for that item.
  3. Move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous / Next buttons before you submit.
  4. Read each stem carefully. Where it asks for the best, first or most likely option, several answers will be partly right and some are true statements that do not answer the question asked.
  5. After submitting you get your score, a marked answer sheet, and an explanation for every question — including why each near-miss option fails.
