Neurological Physiotherapy Competency Check

A 30-item assessment for physiotherapy staff working with neurological caseloads: motor control and motor learning theory, the ICF framework, the named treatment approaches, stroke assessment and rehabilitation, neurological examination, spinal cord injury, brain injury and disorders of consciousness, movement disorders and the cerebellum, and the demyelinating and neuromuscular conditions. Twenty-two items test knowledge directly; eight are clinical vignettes. This is a hard paper by design — on most items more than one option is defensible, and you are being asked for the strongest one.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (A–E) and every item is single best answer — choose exactly one.
  2. Move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous / Next buttons before you submit. Any question left unanswered is marked incorrect.
  3. Watch the polarity of the stem. Several items ask which statement is FALSE, incorrect, NOT associated or least appropriate — on those, four options are true and you are looking for the one that is not.
  4. Where a stem asks for the best or most likely option, several answers will be partly right and some are true statements that do not answer the question asked.
  5. Sides and levels matter. Where a stem names a right or left lesion, or a spinal level, work out the consequence before you read the options.
  6. After submitting you get your score, a marked answer sheet, a topic breakdown, and an explanation for every question — including why each near-miss option fails.
