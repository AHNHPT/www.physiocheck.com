Paediatric Physiotherapy Competency Check

A 30-item assessment for physiotherapy staff working with infants, children and adolescents: neurodisability and cerebral palsy, developmental surveillance, paediatric orthopaedics and the limping child, neuromuscular disease, paediatric respiratory care, and the musculoskeletal presentations of growth. Twelve items test knowledge directly; eighteen are clinical vignettes. This is a hard paper by design — on most items more than one option is defensible, and you are being asked for the strongest one.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (A–E). Most require one answer; items marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item is marked as incorrect for that item.
  3. Move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous / Next buttons before you submit.
  4. Read each stem carefully. Where it asks for the best, first or most likely option, several answers will be partly right and some are true statements that do not answer the question asked.
  5. Ages matter. Several items turn on whether a finding is normal for that age — check the age in the stem before you answer.
  6. After submitting you get your score, a marked answer sheet, a topic breakdown, and an explanation for every question — including why each near-miss option fails.
