Paediatric Physiotherapy Competency Check

A 30-item self-assessment for junior physiotherapy staff, covering paediatric knowledge and case-based clinical reasoning across neurodevelopmental, musculoskeletal, respiratory, and professional practice topics encountered in day-to-day paediatric caseloads. Fifteen items test core knowledge; fifteen present clinical scenarios.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (1–5). Most questions require you to select one answer; questions marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item will be marked as incorrect for that item.
  3. You may move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous/Next buttons before submitting.
  4. Once you submit, you'll see your score, a marked answer sheet, and a full explanation for every question.
