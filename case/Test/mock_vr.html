Vestibular Rehabilitation Competency Check

A 30-item assessment for physiotherapy staff running a vestibular caseload: labyrinthine anatomy and reflex physiology, the BPPV variants and their repositioning maneuvers, acute unilateral and bilateral hypofunction, the peripheral-versus-central decision, exercise prescription and dosage, and the outcome measures used to prove it worked. Eighteen items test knowledge directly; twelve are clinical vignettes. This is a hard paper by design — on most items more than one option is defensible, and you are being asked for the strongest one.

Before you begin

  1. Each question has five options (1–5). Most require one answer; items marked “Select 2” require exactly two.
  2. Selecting the wrong number of answers on a “Select 2” item is marked as incorrect for that item.
  3. Move freely between questions using the number rail or the Previous / Next buttons before you submit.
  4. Sides matter. Where a stem names a right or left ear, or a direction of nystagmus, work the side out before you read the options — two options will usually name the right treatment on the wrong side.
  5. Read each stem carefully. Where it asks for the best, first or most likely option, several answers will be partly right and some are true statements that do not answer the question asked.
  6. After submitting you get your score, a marked answer sheet, a topic breakdown, and an explanation for every question — including why each near-miss option fails.
