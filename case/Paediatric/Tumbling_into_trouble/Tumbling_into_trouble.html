Tumbling into Trouble

Paediatric OPD

Assessment Management

Evelyn, an 8-year-old girl, was referred by AED for neck physiotherapy 3 days ago. Today is her 1st OPD physio session; she is brought by her mother.

Referral: AED → OPD neck physio

HPI:

  • ? Neck sprain when jumping on the trampoline at the clubhouse playhouse
  • Tried to do a basic flip, landed awkwardly on her shoulder/neck, ? felt a sudden "pop"
  • Developed mild neck pain immediately
  • Her domestic helper brought her home and reported to her mother
  • Mother thought it might be muscle strain only, as Evelyn complained of mild neck pain only
  • However, her neck pain worsened and became rigidly locked in a twisted position later at night
  • Parents then brought her to AED

AED notes:

Neck sprain after jumping on trampoline
Neck tilted in torticollis
4 limbs power full, walk unaided
XR C spine lateral view: no obvious fracture
Mx: DC home, refer OPD neck physio

XR C spine lateral view in AED:

SHx:

  • Good past health
  • Studying P3 in mainstream primary school
  • Lives with parents and the domestic helper

PMHx:

  • Nil significant past medical history

Subjective Exam today:

Evelyn reported that her neck was less painful now, but felt some stiffness when moving her neck.

Observation:

Neck tilted to left side, rotated to right side, and slightly flexed forward

P/E:

Resting posture
Neck in 20° left side flexion and 30° right rotation
