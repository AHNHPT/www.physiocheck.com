PneumoSim · Case 01

Airway clearance in severe pneumonia A real-time ICU simulation for physiotherapists. Assess, read the film, plan, and suction a deteriorating patient. No worry, your performance will not be judged.

Respiratory · ICU Endotracheal suction ~20 min Scored · 9 stations

Mr Chan Kwok-Wah, 68, day 3 of invasive ventilation for severe community-acquired pneumonia. The nurse has bleeped you: he is rattly, his peak pressures are climbing and his saturations are drifting. You have a ventilated patient, a bedside monitor and a decision to make.

Everything you do moves his physiology in real time. Suction too long, too deep, or without pre-oxygenating and you will watch it happen on the trace.
