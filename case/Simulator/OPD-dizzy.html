University footballer, fit, no medical history, no regular medication. She is in the criterion-based return-to-running block of her programme, and this morning's session was a twelve-minute progressive treadmill run with strength and hop testing beforehand.

Her appointment was at 08:30. She came straight from home in Tai Po, and she has not eaten since dinner last night. At twelve minutes she thumbed the speed down and stepped straight off the belt, and told you she felt dizzy and a bit sick. You took her arm and sat her in the chair for a brief rest.

Two minutes later her head dropped and her eyes rolled up. She was completely unresponsive for about ten seconds — no stiffening, no jerking, no incontinence — and then opened her eyes and apologised. She is grey, she is sweating, and she says she still feels a bit dizzy and weak.
