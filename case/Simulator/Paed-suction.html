PaedSim 01 · Paediatric respiratory

The nasopharyngeal suction station A 4-year-old with chronic lung disease of prematurity, spastic quadriplegic CP and hMPV bronchiolitis. You have a catheter, a vacuum dial and a child who cannot clear his own secretions. Everything you do to him happens in real time.

Nasopharyngeal suction Ex-25 weeker · BPD CP GMFCS V PEG fed hMPV 20–30 min

Suction is one of the few things a physiotherapist does that can kill a child in under a minute. Too shallow and you achieve nothing. Too deep and he vomits his feed into his own larynx. Too long and you watch the saturation fall. The technique is the whole treatment.

You will measure him, choose a catheter, set a vacuum pressure, then drag the catheter into his nostril yourself and hold the thumb port to apply suction — exactly as you would occlude the port with your thumb at the bedside.

Headphones recommended — the monitor tone drops in pitch as he desaturates.
