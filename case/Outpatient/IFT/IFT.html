Treatment given

After proceeding the above procedures, you then start the IFT over his left knee with the following parameters:

  • 4 suction cups crossing left knee joint
  • Intermittent suction force with low vacuum level
  • Carrier Frequency: 4000 Hz
  • Beat Frequency: 120 - 200 Hz
  • Sweep mode
  • Intensity: 15 mA
  • Duration: 15 minutes

🧪 Learning points

1. Continuous suction is strongly discouraged as it could increase the risk for skin damage, bruising, and severe electrical or thermal burns.
FeatureIntermittent Suction (Recommended)Continuous Suction (High Risk)
Tissue PerfusionCyclic rebound hyperemia maintains blood flow.Stagnant blood flow / localized ischemia.
Thermal DissipationPreserved via healthy capillary blood flow.Reduced heat sink effect → thermal buildup.
Moisture RetentionUniform sponge moisture maintained.Rapid drying → high-density "hot spots".
Tissue IntegrityMassaging effect prevents mechanical shear.High risk of petechiae, bruising, and skin breakdown.
2. While adequately dampening pad sponges is essential for basic electrical conduction, simply adding more water (over-saturating the sponges) will not lower the risk of skin burns.
FactorDamp Sponge (Standard)Over-Saturated Sponge
Capillary Blood FlowMaintained only if suction is intermittent.Stopped (ischemia suppresses thermal dissipation).
Fluid BehaviorEvenly distributed moisture.Squeezed to edges → current density spikes.
Skin IntegrityIntact stratum corneum barrier.Maceration → patchy drops in impedance & burns.
3. Increasing current intensity during electrotherapy directly elevates the risk of skin burns.
MechanismHow Increased Intensity Causes Burn RiskKey Clinical Takeaway for IFT
Exponential Heat ProductionThermal energy increases with the square of the intensity. Doubling the intensity will increase heat generated at the skin interface.Even a small turn of the intensity dial creates a massive jump in heat production under the electrodes.
Bypassed Warning SystemMedium frequencies (~4000 Hz) bypass normal skin resistance, allowing high current intensity without immediate pain.Patients don't feel the heat building up until thermal tissue damage has already occurred.
Current Hotspots (Current Density)Pushing higher intensity through uneven sponge moisture, dry spots, or electrode edges concentrates energy into tiny skin areas.High intensity turns minor setup flaws (like a dry sponge edge) into localized skin burns.
Sensory Masking & Nerve FatigueHigh intensity rapidly numbs local sensory nerves (accommodation) and activates the Pain Gate Mechanism.The patient's perception of "comfortable tingling" remains unchanged even as skin temperature reaches unsafe levels.
