Why we built this platform

Half of it never happened
during the exercise.

Across 2019-23, 29 serious clinical incidents (AIRS SI≥4)were reviewed. Read together, they do not describe careless treatment. They describe a profession that is most exposed at the moments it is least watching — while setting a patient up, and after the exercise has finished.

14 of 29 — 48% — fell in the preparation stage or the end of the session. Add the three caught on pre-treatment checks and more than half of everything in the review happened outside the window we think of as "the treatment".

Timeline covers the 27 incidents that occurred within a patient treatment episode. Two outside physiotherapy management.

So we built somewhere to meet them first.

Not one of these was a careless physiotherapist. They were competent clinicians in ordinary sessions, meeting a situation they had never had the chance to rehearse. You cannot schedule a fragility fracture or a sudden desaturation for a teaching round. You can build a place where the next generation meets one before a real patient does. That is what PhysioCheck is for.

LESSON 01

Rehearse the whole episode

The risk did not sit inside the exercise. It sat in the set-up before it and the walk-out after it — so the scenarios run the whole session, not the interesting middle.

LESSON 02

Read the trend, not the snapshot

Vitals stable on approach is written into almost every one of these records. The live simulators run physiology forward in real time, so you see the drift — and pay for the delay.

LESSON 03

A checklist grades risk, it does not remove it

Screening was completed correctly in cases that still went wrong. Every challenge makes you commit to a decision under uncertainty, then shows the reasoning behind the mark.
