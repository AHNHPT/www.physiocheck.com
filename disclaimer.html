Welcome to PhysioCheck. It is a teaching platform — not a clinical reference, and nothing in it is advice about a real patient. Please read this carefully before using it.

01 Educational purpose only

PhysioCheck is designed as a clinical reasoning challenge platform for students and staff. The cases, scenarios and feedback provided are for educational and professional development purposes only. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment plans for actual patients.

The information presented may not be comprehensive, up to date, or applicable to every individual situation. All cases are anonymised, fictionalised, or based on generalised experiences, and are not intended to represent real patients or specific medical conditions.

02 Clinical judgment

While the scenarios are based on clinical principles, users must exercise their own professional judgment when treating real-world patients. Guidelines and evidence-based practices are constantly evolving; therefore the information within this platform should not be used as a sole reference for clinical decision-making. Reliance on the content of this platform for medical decision-making is at your own risk.
